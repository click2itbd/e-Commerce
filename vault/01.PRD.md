# 01. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
**Click2IT BD — Unified E-Commerce Storefront, Retail POS & Enterprise ERP Platform**
An all-in-one technology retail e-commerce website, PC Builder, Domain & Hosting portal, and full-suite ERP/Accounting Admin Dashboard.

---

## Core Modules & Functional Requirements

### 1. Public E-Commerce Storefront
- **Product Catalog & Discovery:** Multi-level categories (`Category` -> `SubCategory` -> `SubSubCategory`), brand filtering, specification filtering, flash sales, featured items, and search.
- **Stock & Availability Rules:**
  - Products with `stock === 0` remain **available for purchase/order** on the website unless `isOutOfStock === true` is explicitly checked by Admin.
  - Only when `isOutOfStock === true` is checked in the Admin panel does the storefront show **"OUT OF STOCK"** and block adding to cart / checkout.
- **Interactive 3D / Standard PC Builder:** Component selection with socket/RAM/TDP compatibility validation, quotation saving, and direct ordering.
- **Domain & Web Hosting Portal:** TLD search, domain registration/transfer, hosting plan selection, and billing cycle configuration.
- **Customer Profile & Smart Address Book:**
  - Supports full Bangladesh geographic hierarchy: `Division` -> `District` -> `Thana/Upazila` -> `Union` (with automatic postcode mapping).
  - Auto-fills saved default address on Checkout; provides an address selector dropdown when 2 or more addresses are saved.

### 2. Admin ERP, POS & Sales System
- **Click POS & Sales Form (`SalesForm.tsx`, `POS/`):**
  - Supports regular sales, quotations, challans, and invoice editing.
  - Serial number tracking (`availableSerials` / `selectedSerials`) and warranty duration per item.
  - Branch / Project tagging per customer order.
  - Document numbering (`INV-XXXXX`, `QUO-XXXXX`, `CHL-XXXXX`) with collision protection.
- **Inventory & Stock Deduction Rules:**
  - Online order placement (`pending`) does **not** immediately deduct admin stock.
  - Stock is deducted once when order status transitions to `processing` (or `delivered`/`completed` if not yet deducted), tracked via `stockDeducted: true`.
  - Cancelling or returning an order restores stock if `stockDeducted` was true.

### 3. Double-Entry Accounting & Party Ledger (`Ledger.tsx`, `CustomerDueList.tsx`)
- **Individual Customer vs Corporate Statement Rule:**
  - Selecting an individual customer (e.g., `Simpletree Hena Kunjo`) in Ledger or Customer Due History **must only** show transactions and unpaid bills belonging to that specific customer.
  - Branch/Project filtering is shown for a customer **only** if that customer has branches/projects added under their profile or orders.
  - Full corporate company rollups (combining all sister entities) only occur when explicitly selecting an entry from the **Corporate Companies** section (`isCompany: true`).
- **Financial Reports:** General Ledger, DayBook, Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Account Statements, and Expense/Income tracking.

### 4. Business Units & Corporate Projects (`BusinessUnitsTab.tsx`)
- Dedicated management for corporate clients and business units (`main`, `simpletree`, `spacezero`, `volumezero`, etc.), work orders, project budgets, and bill-wise due settlement.
