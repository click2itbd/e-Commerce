# 03. Design Tokens & UI Conventions

## Brand Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Accent (Storefront Orange):** `#F97316` (`orange-500`) / `#EA580C` (`orange-600`)
- **Admin Primary Dark Navy:** `#081621` to `#122b3f` (Sidebar, Header Banners, Statement Headers)
- **Admin Action Red:** `#EF4444` (`red-500`) / `#DC2626` (`red-600`)
- **Semantic Status Colors:**
  - **Success / Paid / Credit:** `#10B981` (`emerald-500`), bg `emerald-50`, text `emerald-700`
  - **Warning / Partial / Due:** `#F59E0B` (`amber-500`), bg `amber-50`, text `amber-700`
  - **Danger / Out of Stock / Unpaid:** `#EF4444` (`red-500`), bg `red-50`, text `red-700`
  - **Info / Corporate / Branch Tag:** `#3B82F6` (`blue-500`), bg `blue-50`, text `blue-700`

---

## Typography & Formatting
- **Currency Formatting:** Always use `formatCurrency(amount, settings)` (defaults to `BDT 1,234.00` or `৳1,234`).
- **Headings & KPI Values:** `font-black` or `font-bold` with tight tracking (`tracking-tight`).
- **Labels & Badges:** `text-[10px]` or `text-xs`, `font-bold` / `font-black`, `uppercase`, `tracking-wider`.

---

## Component Patterns
- **Cards & Containers:** `bg-white rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl border border-gray-200/80 shadow-xs`.
- **Modals:** `fixed inset-0 bg-black/60 backdrop-blur-xs z-50 flex items-center justify-center p-4` with `rounded-2xl shadow-2xl`.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Always include `cursor-pointer transition-all` and disabled state styling (`disabled:opacity-50`).
